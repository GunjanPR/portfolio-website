How I think

Reliability is
a design choice.

The same four principles show up across every system I build. They turn vague claims about intelligence into engineering decisions that can be inspected.

01 / Instrument

Measure the system, not just the model.

Latency, cost, throughput, failures, and traces belong in the design from day one.

02 / Evaluate

Define success before chasing it.

Named baselines and reproducible workloads make improvement concrete.

03 / Bound

Capability needs explicit control.

Permissions, uncertainty, approval, and abstention are core system behavior.

04 / Iterate

Let evidence change the architecture.

Benchmarks are feedback loops, not a final slide added after the build.
